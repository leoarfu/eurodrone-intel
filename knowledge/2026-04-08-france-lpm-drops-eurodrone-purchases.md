# France drops Eurodrone and Patroller purchases in updated military programming law
Date: 2026-04-08
Source: Aerotime
URL: https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/france-patroller-eurodrone-dropped-lpm-update
---
France confirmed cancellation of planned Eurodrone and Safran Patroller purchases in an updated military programming law (LPM) presented to the Council of Ministers on April 8, 2026, which adds €36 billion to the existing €413 billion 2024–2030 defense blueprint (source: https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/france-patroller-eurodrone-dropped-lpm-update). The bill states MALE-class drone requirements have been "reoriented" toward lower-cost sovereign theater drones, describing Eurodrone as less suited to high-intensity conflict (source: https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/france-patroller-eurodrone-dropped-lpm-update). Eurodrone had completed its Critical Design Review in October 2025, with first flight then scheduled for January 2027 and first delivery targeted for 2030 (source: https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/france-patroller-eurodrone-dropped-lpm-update). Note: subsequent Airbus statements at the June 2026 ILA Berlin air show characterized France as "remaining committed to the programme" while deferring order timing, so the practical status is a funding deferral through 2035 rather than a confirmed formal withdrawal — reporting on this point is not fully consistent across sources (source: https://meta-defense.fr/en/2026/06/18/eurodrone-france-confirms-commitment-may-delay-order/).
