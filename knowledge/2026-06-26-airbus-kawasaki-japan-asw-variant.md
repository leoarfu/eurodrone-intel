# Airbus and Kawasaki sign MoU on Japanese ASW variant of Eurodrone
Date: 2026-06-26
Source: okdiario / Meta-Defense
URL: https://okdiario.com/techy/en/europe-wanted-to-prove-that-it-was-capable-of-manufacturing-its-own-large-military-drone-without-relying-so-heavily-on-the-united-states-or-israel-but-the-eurodrone-has-ended-up-facing-delays-funding/5816/
---
On June 26, 2026, Airbus signed a memorandum of understanding with Kawasaki Heavy Industries to explore a Japanese anti-submarine warfare (ASW) variant of the U950 Eurodrone (source: https://okdiario.com/techy/en/europe-wanted-to-prove-that-it-was-capable-of-manufacturing-its-own-large-military-drone-without-relying-so-heavily-on-the-united-states-or-israel-but-the-eurodrone-has-ended-up-facing-delays-funding/5816/). Japan has held observer status in the Eurodrone program since 2023 (source: https://okdiario.com/techy/en/europe-wanted-to-prove-that-it-was-capable-of-manufacturing-its-own-large-military-drone-without-relying-so-heavily-on-the-united-states-or-israel-but-the-eurodrone-has-ended-up-facing-delays-funding/5816/). The move signals Airbus pursuing export and maritime-mission diversification for the program at the same time one of its original European launch customers, France, is deferring its own purchases (source: https://okdiario.com/techy/en/europe-wanted-to-prove-that-it-was-capable-of-manufacturing-its-own-large-military-drone-without-relying-so-heavily-on-the-united-states-or-israel-but-the-eurodrone-has-ended-up-facing-delays-funding/5816/). Eurodrone's baseline design already advertises multi-mission flexibility extending to naval and anti-submarine warfare roles beyond its core ISTAR mission (source: https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/defence/uas/u950-eurodrone).
