# Eurodrone (U950/MALE RPAS) program overview
Date: 2026-08-15
Source: Airbus / OCCAR / Wikipedia (background compilation)
URL: https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/defence/uas/u950-eurodrone
---
Eurodrone (Airbus designation U950) is a twin-turboprop Medium Altitude Long Endurance Remotely Piloted Aircraft System (MALE RPAS) developed jointly by Germany, France, Italy, and Spain, managed by OCCAR (source: https://occar.int/our-work/programmes/male-rpas-medium-altitude-long-endurance-remotely-piloted-aircraft-system). Airbus Defence and Space (Germany) is industry prime, with Airbus DS S.A.U. (Spain), Dassault Aviation (France), and Leonardo (Italy) as major subcontractors (source: https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/defence/uas/u950-eurodrone). The February 2022 contract covers 20 systems (60 aircraft, 40 ground control stations) valued at roughly €7.1 billion (source: https://meta-defense.fr/en/2026/07/02/eurodrone-delay-airbus-dassault-tensions/). The aircraft uses two General Electric Catalyst turboprop engines in a pusher configuration, is designed for ISTAR missions with growth to naval ASW and AEW roles, and is the first RPAS natively designed for certified operation in non-segregated civil airspace under NATO STANAG 4671 (source: https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/defence/uas/u950-eurodrone). India and Japan hold observer status in the program (source: https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/stories/2025-09-eurodrone-europes-sovereign-skies).
