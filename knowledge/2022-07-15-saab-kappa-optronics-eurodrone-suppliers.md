# Airbus secures Saab flight management computers and Kappa Optronics vision systems for Eurodrone
Date: 2022-07-15
Source: Avionics International
URL: https://www.aviationtoday.com/2022/07/15/airbus-secures-flight-management-computers-vision-systems-suppliers-for-eurodrone/
---
Airbus Defence and Space secured supplier agreements with Saab for modular flight management and airspace integration computers, and with Kappa Optronics for vision systems, both for the Eurodrone program (source: https://www.aviationtoday.com/2022/07/15/airbus-secures-flight-management-computers-vision-systems-suppliers-for-eurodrone/). These followed the February 2022 global contract in which OCCAR awarded Airbus Defence and Space the industry-prime role, with Airbus DS S.A.U (Spain), Dassault Aviation (France), and Leonardo (Italy) as major subcontractors, covering 20 systems and five years of initial in-service support built entirely on European technologies (source: https://www.aviationtoday.com/2022/07/15/airbus-secures-flight-management-computers-vision-systems-suppliers-for-eurodrone/).
