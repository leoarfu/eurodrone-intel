# Dassault demands compensation from Airbus over Eurodrone workshare
Date: 2026-06-12
Source: Reuters (via Pravda France, Defense News)
URL: https://www.thedefensenews.com/Dassault-Seeks-Compensation-From-Airbus-as-Eurodrone-Dispute-Erupts-After-FCAS-Collapse/
---
Dassault Aviation is seeking compensation from Airbus after France's reduced Eurodrone purchases cut into Dassault's expected industrial workshare under the program's "geo-return" principle (source: https://www.thedefensenews.com/Dassault-Seeks-Compensation-From-Airbus-as-Eurodrone-Dispute-Erupts-After-FCAS-Collapse/). The dispute surfaced days after France and Germany acknowledged the collapse of the separate Future Combat Air System (FCAS) fighter program on June 8, 2026 (source: https://aerohaber.com/how-a-dassault-airbus-workshare-fight-killed-europes-116-billion-6th-generation-fighter-program/). At the ILA Berlin Air Show, eight German aerospace companies led by Airbus formed a "Team Gen 6" alliance for future fighter development without Dassault, deepening the rift (source: https://www.thedefensenews.com/Dassault-Seeks-Compensation-From-Airbus-as-Eurodrone-Dispute-Erupts-After-FCAS-Collapse/). By early July, Dassault CEO Eric Trappier told a French Senate committee that Airbus had told Dassault to leave the Eurodrone program, and that programme-level relations had broken down entirely (source: https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/dassault-confirms-fresh-rift-airbus-over-eurodrone/). Airbus declined to comment on Trappier's remarks (source: https://thedefensepost.com/2026/07/06/dassault-airbus-eurodrone-rift/).
